INDULGE IN THE BLISS OF AN URBAN-RESORT CLUBHOUSE

奢雅會所 展現生活格調

CLUB ATLAS1, the prestigious residents' clubhouse,
spans a total indoor and landscaped area of over 83,000 square feet2,
jointly crafted by internationally acclaimed interior design team ARK3 and landscape visionaries OTHERLAND3.

 

The clubhouse design is centred on the core concept of "LIVING WITH THE SEA",
weaving together three major themes: "Ocean, Family, Health".
It skilfully incorporates  flowing blue curves and wave‑inspired motifs, creating a refined dialogue between geometry and nature.

 

THE ATLAS features a distinctive dual‑clubhouse linked by a 24‑hour4 all-weather pedestrian skybridge4.
The clubhouse brings together over 30 diverse active and leisure amenities1 to cater to the different rhythm of residents’ life.

PREMIER PROVISIONS FOR SPLENDID LIVING

國際頂級廚衛家電 薈萃璀璨生活

Every inch of detail is refined by the designer's meticulous craftsmanship.
The glass curtain walls of the residential units adopts a low-emissivity (Low-E)6 double-glazed glass design,
combining exceptional thermal insulation, acoustic performance, and energy efficiency.  

 

The residential units are equipped with international-grade kitchen and bathroom appliances,
including MIELE hobs and refrigerators7 (Germany), GAGGENAU washer-dryers7 (Germany), KOHLER bathroom fittings7 (USA),
DAIKIN air-conditioners7 (Japan) and PANASONIC bathroom thermo ventilators7 (Japan) — curating an extraordinary lifestyle from the inside out.
